# A new look for the desktop app

A smaller, tighter window; relay and latency in the menu bar.

## Sign-in
- No more passwords: enter your email and the code sent to it
- Signing in and signing up are one step; a new address gets an account
- Two-factor authentication can be turned on in your account settings

## Window
- Sidebar navigation: Overview, Sharing, Pairing, Activity, Feedback, Settings
- A status bar with the connection, the relay in use and its latency
- Latency is coloured green, amber or red by how fast it is

## Menu bar
- The menu shows the relay in use, its latency, sharing and your available credit
- Linux now has the icon too (needs Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13 or later)

## Updates
- A new release comes with a one-line description; open it for the full notes
